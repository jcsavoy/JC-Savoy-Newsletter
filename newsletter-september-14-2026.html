Dressmaker Relocates to Lighthouse Cottage; Keeper "Was Informed," Says Ogle

CLIFFHAVEN — Following a gas leak that closed her shop with seventeen festival costumes still unfinished, Jamie Crenshaw has moved her entire operation — bolts, forms, pins, and the small machine she keeps at her apartment — into the historic lighthouse cottage. Harriet Ogle arranged it within the hour. "That cottage has good light and nobody using it," she said.

The cottage does, in fact, have somebody using it. Trevor Morrison has kept the light for four years and was described by neighbors as "a man who likes a schedule." Asked whether Mr. Morrison had been consulted, Mrs. Ogle said he had been informed, which she considers the same thing, and then changed the subject to the costumes.

As of Thursday, three dress forms stand in the front room, one of them wearing a hat. Mr. Morrison has been seen on the porch at odd hours. The Gazette asked him for comment. He said the deadline is six weeks. He said it twice.

Dolly's Healthy Blonde Brownies

A Sneaky-Healthy Treat · Makes 9

Ingredients • 14-oz can navy beans, drained
• 4 pinches stevia
• 2 Tbl olive oil
• 1 tsp baking soda
• 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
• ¼ tsp sea salt
• ½ tsp cinnamon
• ½ tsp pure vanilla extract
• 2 eggs
• ½ cup rolled oats
• ½ cup dark chocolate chips Directions 1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Blend all the ingredients except the chocolate chips, in the order listed, pulsing in a blender between each addition.
3. Once the batter is very smooth, add the chocolate chips and mix in gently.
4. Lightly coat a square baking pan with butter and line with parchment, then pour in the batter.
5. Bake at 350°F for 35–40 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
6. Let stand 20 minutes before slicing into squares.

The Lighthouse Invasion

She invaded his silence. He built her a reason to stay. Trevor Morrison likes his life quiet, orderly, and free of women who sing the wrong lyrics to every song and leave pins in his butter dish. Six weeks, that's all she needs.
