Harbormaster Stu Predicts "Romantic Turbulence" Through August; Pete's Bar Immediately Opens a Betting Pool

Cliffhaven's self-appointed romantic authority, Harbormaster Stu, announced this week that the harbor "has a feeling" about several unnamed local singles and their upcoming summers. "The tides don't lie," Stu reportedly declared to three tourists and a seagull on Tuesday. By Wednesday morning, Pete had already written the names on a chalkboard behind the bar and was accepting wagers at two-to-one odds. Residents are advised to proceed with their hearts as usual — and perhaps avoid eye contact with either man.

— Your Correspondent on the Cliffhaven Docks

Cozy Cowboy Coffee Cake

A Kingsley Family Favorite

You'll Need 2 large eggs  ·  1 cup vegetable oil (or applesauce)  ·  1¼ cups milk  ·  1 tbsp vanilla extract  ·  3 cups all-purpose flour  ·  1 cup granulated sugar  ·  1 tbsp baking powder  ·  ½ tsp salt
Streusel: 1¼ cups light brown sugar  ·  1 tbsp ground cinnamon  ·  ¼-⅓ cup unsalted butter (melted) To Make It Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9×13 pan. Whisk wet ingredients; in a separate bowl, whisk dry ingredients. Combine and pour half into the pan. Mix brown sugar and cinnamon; sprinkle half over batter. Layer the rest of the batter, then remaining sugar mix, and drizzle with melted butter. Bake 35–40 minutes.

This is the cake Zane Kingsley keeps finding on his porch every Sunday morning. He suspects Raven. He's not wrong. (Optional: Fold in 1½ cups of blueberries for an extra treat!)
